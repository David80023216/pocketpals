About Pocket Pals 🐾

A game about love (and snacks)

Pocket Pals is a free virtual pet game. Adopt a magical creature, keep it fed, happy, and rested, and earn its trust. Care for it well and it will evolve from a tiny baby into a magnificent adult.

Tips for great pal parents

🌡️ Needs drain in real time — even while you're away. Check in a few times a day!

✨ Thriving bonus — keep every need above 75 and all coin rewards double.

🎁 Surprises — pals sometimes find gifts while you play. The more you play, the more surprises.

🔥 Streaks — visit daily to grow your streak and earn bigger coin rewards.

💎 Gems — earned by leveling up, streaks, and lucky finds. They buy fixed upgrades only, never random draws.

Fair play

Everything in Pocket Pals is original — the pals, the art, the world. The full game is free; coins are earned by playing, and purchased gems unlock only guaranteed, non-random perks. Virtual items have no cash value.

Privacy

Privacy

Your game saves in your own browser (localStorage) and plays fully offline. If you sign in with Google, your pals also sync to a secure cloud save so they follow you across devices, and you can add friends, send gifts, and appear on the friends leaderboard. We never sell data or show your info to anyone except players you add as friends. Signing in is always optional — if you clear your browser data without signing in, your pals go with it, so visit often! 💕
